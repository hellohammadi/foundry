# Customers don't come back (high churn)
topic: retention

## Symptoms
- Many first-time buyers, few repeat buyers
- Subscriptions cancelled after one or two months
- Growth stalls despite marketing spend

## Root causes
- The product doesn't deliver the promised result quickly enough
- Poor onboarding, so customers never get value
- No reason or reminder to return
- Attracting the wrong customers with misleading ads or deep discounts

## How to confirm
- Calculate monthly churn and repeat-purchase rate
- Call five customers who left and ask why
- Compare churn by acquisition channel

## Fixes
- Define the first moment of value and get every customer there fast
- Add a welcome sequence and check-ins
- Create loyalty rewards or subscription perks
- Stop channels that bring customers who churn fastest

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Interview 5 churned customers and measure churn by channel
- Week 2: Redesign onboarding around the first moment of value
- Week 3: Launch a welcome email sequence and a win-back offer
- Week 4: Add a loyalty perk and review churn again

## Metrics
- Monthly churn rate
- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer lifetime value
