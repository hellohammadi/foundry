# Social media isn't bringing customers
topic: marketing

## Symptoms
- Posting regularly but low reach and engagement
- Followers don't turn into customers
- Unsure what to post

## Root causes
- Posting about the product instead of the customer's problem
- No consistent content pillars or format
- No clear call to action or path to purchase
- Wrong platform for the audience

## How to confirm
- Review the last 20 posts: how many teach, entertain or show proof?
- Check which posts drove profile visits or link clicks

## Fixes
- Use 3–4 content pillars: teach, behind the scenes, proof, offer
- Lead with hooks about the customer's problem
- Use short video where the platform rewards it
- Add one clear call to action per post and a link that converts

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Audit past posts and define content pillars
- Week 2: Batch-create 10 short videos with strong hooks
- Week 3: Post daily and reply to every comment within an hour
- Week 4: Double down on the top three formats and add a lead magnet

## Metrics
- Reach and saves per post
- Profile visits to link clicks
- Customers who came from social
