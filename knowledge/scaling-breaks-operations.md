# Growth is breaking operations
topic: operations

## Symptoms
- More orders means more mistakes and complaints
- Delivery times getting longer
- Team constantly firefighting

## Root causes
- Processes that worked at 10 orders a day fail at 100
- Tools and spreadsheets that don't connect
- No clear owner for each part of the process

## How to confirm
- Map the order-to-delivery process and mark where errors happen
- Count complaints by cause

## Fixes
- Standardise the process with checklists
- Connect tools with automation (orders → inventory → shipping → email)
- Give every step one owner
- Temporarily slow growth to fix quality if needed

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Map the process and list the top error causes
- Week 2: Fix and document the top error cause
- Week 3: Automate the hand-off between two tools
- Week 4: Assign owners and set a weekly quality review

## Metrics
- Error and complaint rate
- Delivery time
- Orders handled per person
