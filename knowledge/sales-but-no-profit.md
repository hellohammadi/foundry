# Sales but no profit
topic: pricing

## Symptoms
- Revenue is growing but the bank balance is not
- Busy every day, yet the founder can't pay themselves
- Discounts and free delivery are used to win most orders

## Root causes
- Prices set below the true cost per order (product + packaging + delivery + payment fees + returns)
- Heavy discounting that trained customers to wait for offers
- Best-selling products are the lowest-margin ones
- Fixed costs (rent, salaries, tools) grew faster than gross margin

## How to confirm
- Calculate contribution margin per order: price minus every variable cost
- Rank products by margin, not by sales volume
- Check what share of orders used a discount code

## Fixes
- Raise prices 10–20% on low-margin items and test the impact on conversion
- Set a minimum order value or charge for delivery below it
- Replace blanket discounts with bundles that raise order value
- Push high-margin products in marketing and on the homepage
- Cut or renegotiate the top three fixed costs

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Build a unit-economics sheet for every product and find loss-makers
- Week 2: Raise prices on loss-makers and add a free-delivery threshold
- Week 3: Launch a bundle offer and stop site-wide discounts
- Week 4: Review margin per order and renegotiate one supplier or fixed cost

## Metrics
- Contribution margin per order
- Average order value
- Net profit margin
