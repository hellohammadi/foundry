# Cash flow crunch
topic: cash flow

## Symptoms
- Profitable on paper but can't pay bills on time
- Waiting on customers to pay invoices
- Stock or supplier payments due before customers pay

## Root causes
- Long payment terms for customers, short terms with suppliers
- Too much cash tied up in inventory
- No cash forecast, so shortfalls are a surprise
- Large one-off spending without a buffer

## How to confirm
- List money owed to you and by you with due dates
- Build a 13-week cash forecast
- Measure days sales outstanding (how long customers take to pay)

## Fixes
- Invoice immediately and shorten payment terms; offer a small early-payment discount
- Take deposits or upfront payment for large orders
- Negotiate longer terms with suppliers
- Reduce slow-moving stock
- Keep a cash buffer of at least two months of fixed costs

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Build a 13-week cash forecast and chase every overdue invoice
- Week 2: Change terms to deposits or upfront payment for new customers
- Week 3: Ask your top two suppliers for 30 extra days
- Week 4: Clear slow-moving stock and start a cash buffer account

## Metrics
- Weeks of cash runway
- Days sales outstanding
- Cash conversion cycle
