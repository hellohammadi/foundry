# Struggling to make the first hire
topic: hiring

## Symptoms
- Overwhelmed but unsure who to hire first
- Previous hires didn't work out
- Worried about affording a salary

## Root causes
- Hiring for a vague role instead of a clear outcome
- Hiring a generalist when the bottleneck is specific
- No trial period or onboarding plan

## How to confirm
- Identify the single task that most limits revenue growth
- Estimate how much revenue freeing that time would unlock

## Fixes
- Write a role around one measurable outcome
- Start with a paid trial project or part-time contract
- Prepare a one-week onboarding plan and written processes
- Consider revenue-share or commission for sales roles

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Identify the bottleneck and write an outcome-based role
- Week 2: Post the role and shortlist candidates with a small paid task
- Week 3: Run a two-week trial with the best candidate
- Week 4: Review results and agree a longer-term arrangement

## Metrics
- Hours freed for the founder
- Revenue influenced by the hire
- Time to productivity
