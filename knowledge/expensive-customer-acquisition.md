# Customer acquisition is too expensive
topic: marketing

## Symptoms
- Ads bring customers but cost more than they spend
- Marketing budget keeps rising for the same results
- Growth stops when ads are paused

## Root causes
- Relying on one paid channel
- Customer lifetime value too low to support ad costs
- Weak conversion on the landing page wastes paid traffic
- No organic, referral or word-of-mouth engine

## How to confirm
- Calculate customer acquisition cost (CAC) per channel
- Compare CAC with customer lifetime value (LTV); aim for LTV at least 3x CAC
- Check landing page conversion rate

## Fixes
- Improve conversion before buying more traffic
- Launch a referral programme that rewards both sides
- Invest in one organic channel: content, community or SEO
- Increase LTV with repeat purchases, subscriptions or upsells

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Calculate CAC and LTV per channel and pause the worst channel
- Week 2: Fix the top three conversion problems on the landing page
- Week 3: Launch a give-get referral offer
- Week 4: Publish content consistently on one organic channel

## Metrics
- CAC by channel
- LTV to CAC ratio
- Landing page conversion rate
