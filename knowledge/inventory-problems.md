# Inventory problems (too much or too little stock)
topic: operations

## Symptoms
- Cash stuck in unsold stock
- Best sellers frequently out of stock
- Throwing away expired or outdated products

## Root causes
- Ordering by gut feel instead of sales data
- Minimum order quantities forcing overbuying
- No reorder points

## How to confirm
- Calculate stock turnover per product
- List products with more than 90 days of stock

## Fixes
- Set reorder points from average weekly sales and supplier lead time
- Clear slow movers with bundles or discounts
- Negotiate smaller, more frequent orders
- Offer pre-orders for new products before buying stock

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Calculate stock turnover and find slow movers
- Week 2: Set reorder points for top 10 products
- Week 3: Bundle or discount slow movers
- Week 4: Negotiate smaller order quantities with one supplier

## Metrics
- Stock turnover
- Stock-out rate
- Cash tied up in inventory
