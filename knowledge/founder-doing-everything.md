# Founder doing everything (burnout)
topic: operations

## Symptoms
- Working 12+ hours a day on repetitive tasks
- Business stops when the founder takes a day off
- No time for strategy or sales

## Root causes
- No documented processes, so nothing can be delegated
- Repetitive tasks done by hand that could be automated
- Belief that hiring or outsourcing is unaffordable
- Saying yes to every custom request

## How to confirm
- Log every task for one week and tag it: only-me, delegate, automate, drop
- Calculate your hourly value and compare it with the cost of help

## Fixes
- Automate order confirmations, invoicing, scheduling and FAQs
- Write simple checklists for repeat tasks
- Hire a part-time assistant or freelancer for the most time-consuming task
- Productise services and stop one-off custom work

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Log a week of tasks and tag each one
- Week 2: Automate the top two repetitive tasks
- Week 3: Document three processes as checklists
- Week 4: Delegate one process to a freelancer or assistant

## Metrics
- Founder hours per week on repetitive tasks
- Number of documented processes
- Revenue per founder hour
