# Launched but no customers
topic: traction

## Symptoms
- Product is live but nobody is buying
- Website gets little traffic
- Friends say it's great, strangers don't respond

## Root causes
- Built before validating that people will pay
- Unclear who the customer is, so messaging speaks to nobody
- Waiting for customers to find you instead of going to them
- The offer doesn't clearly solve an urgent problem

## How to confirm
- Can you name 10 specific people who have the problem today?
- Ask 5 potential customers to explain your offer back to you
- Check whether anyone has visited your pricing or checkout page

## Fixes
- Pick one narrow customer segment and talk to 20 of them
- Rewrite the headline around their problem in their words
- Do things that don't scale: direct messages, communities, local outreach
- Offer a pilot or pre-order to get the first paying customers

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Interview 10 target customers about the problem
- Week 2: Rewrite your offer and landing page in their words
- Week 3: Send 50 personal outreach messages and post in 3 communities
- Week 4: Close 3 paying pilot customers and collect feedback

## Metrics
- Customer conversations held
- Outreach reply rate
- Paying customers
