# Visitors but no sales (low website conversion)
topic: marketing

## Symptoms
- Decent traffic but few orders or sign-ups
- High bounce rate
- Many abandoned carts

## Root causes
- Unclear headline: visitors don't understand the offer in 5 seconds
- No trust signals: reviews, guarantees, real photos
- Slow or confusing checkout, surprise costs at the end
- Traffic from the wrong audience

## How to confirm
- Watch five session recordings or ask five strangers to use the site
- Check conversion by traffic source
- Look at where users drop off in the checkout

## Fixes
- Rewrite the headline as outcome + audience + proof
- Add reviews, a guarantee and clear pricing above the fold
- Cut checkout steps and show delivery costs early
- Add abandoned-cart emails

## 30-day starter plan
- Week 1: Run 5 user tests and map drop-off points
- Week 2: Rewrite headline and add trust signals
- Week 3: Simplify checkout and show costs upfront
- Week 4: Launch abandoned-cart emails and compare conversion

## Metrics
- Conversion rate
- Cart abandonment rate
- Bounce rate
